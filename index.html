♫ AI Piano Tutor

LOCAL AI

Ask questions about practice, note reading, rhythm, technique, or Trinity grades. This tutor runs a quantized 1B model in your browser, not through an OpenAI API.

Local model: first use downloads roughly 1 GB of model files and requires a compatible browser with WebGPU and sufficient memory. After loading, chat inference runs on this device. The tutor can review detected note sequences and compare them with typed notes or MusicXML. It cannot yet reliably transcribe full piano audio or read scanned notation. The tutor uses the same generated answer for text and speech. It uses your browser’s built-in speech voice, so there is no second voice model to download.
